Read stat tuples as **HP / Attack / Defense / Sp. Atk / Sp. Def / Speed**. The entry HP column carries the visible or route-derived current/max value. Opponent and ally tuples use the datamined natures, IVs and EVs and Showdown’s species base stats. Player tuples are model inputs. If a model tuple and a recorded maximum disagree, the model uses the entry HP value. An unrecovered HP field is marked **?**.

The player EV ledger accumulates species yields from recorded KOs and captures for eligible party members and adds the four recorded Calcium uses on Kazza. Kazza’s Quiet nature and fixed IVs are 28 / 10 / 9 / 11 / 3 / 31. Chimchar and Monferno use a neutral nature and assumed IVs of 20 / 15 / 15 / 15 / 15 / 25. Machop uses a neutral nature and 15 IVs throughout. Palkia uses a neutral nature and 31 / 20 / 20 / 31 / 20 / 31 as one possible three-perfect-IV spread. The nature and IVs for these three individuals remain unrecovered from the video. Their non-HP stats are model estimates. Kazza’s model estimates still depend on the reconstructed EVs. Move lists follow the video’s level-up and TM choices. Most battle-entry PP counts remain unverified.
