# Snacksworth acquisition and route research

Research date: 2026-11-09. See [the automation plan](snacksworth-plan.md).
Treats are already obtained. Both versions use the same encounter positions.

**Reading this guide:** destinations and landmarks are researched; the selected
flight starts and travel segments are proposed routes, not recorded controller
scripts. Unlock each destination by visiting it if necessary. Assume free mount
flight, swimming and climbing; travel on the surface unless a cave is stated.
Use a north-up map. Named flight points are more useful than guessing camera
directions immediately after teleporting.

## Get the catching team first

### 1. Golduck / 哥达鸭

**Recommended anchor:** North Province (Area One) Pokémon Center, in the northeast
of Paldea. Fly there, open the map, and locate the large nearby lake and its
watchtower. Approach the lake's banks and search for blue Golduck on the shore
or water. This is a bounded wild search, not one guaranteed spawn at the Center.
Catch one, inspect its summary, then return to heal/save. Both versions list
Golduck in this area; typical levels are 47–53.

**Fallback:** Casseroya Lake in northwest Paldea; start from Casseroya Watchtower
No. 2 and search the surrounding shoreline. Choose one small measured search
patch rather than looping the whole lake. If no usable flight anchors are
registered, Psyduck evolved at level 33 is a fallback, not the primary route.

[Species locations](https://www.serebii.net/pokedex-sv/golduck/),
[North Province Area One map](https://www.serebii.net/pokearth/paldea/northprovinceareaone.shtml),
[spawn levels](https://rotomlabs.net/dex/scarlet-violet/golduck)

### 2. Gallade / 艾路雷朵

**First route to scout:** daytime/morning/evening mountain search in North
Province (Area One), using the same Center as Golduck. Move from the lake onto
the surrounding mountain slopes and search a bounded patch for Gallade. It is
rare, so a Pokédex/map icon is not a guaranteed individual. Skip night and switch
to the evolution route if the bounded search budget is exhausted.

**Evolution alternative:** fly to Levincia and search the grassy East Province
(Area Two) outskirts for a **male Kirlia**; verify sex in battle/summary. Fly to
Medali, ascend the hill northwest of town to the ruins, and collect the fixed
Dawn Stone if still present. Use it directly on male Kirlia. This is a candidate
for the preferred automation route if it beats rare-Gallade searches in trials.
Ralts evolved at level 20 is another fallback; a female cannot become Gallade.

**Other wild-search location:** fly to Alfornada, go west to the upper entrance
of Alfornada Cavern, and descend to a measured search patch. No fixed Tera Gallade
was verified here: the cavern's documented Tera encounters are Houndoom and
Umbreon. Do not interpret trainer level tables as fixed wild spawns.

[Cavern map](https://www.serebii.net/pokearth/paldea/alfornadacavern.shtml),
[versions and locations](https://www.serebii.net/pokedex-sv/gallade/),
[random-spawn time restrictions](https://dexcabinet.com/pokemon/gallade),
[Kirlia locations](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Kirlia),
[Dawn Stone ruins route](https://dotesports.com/pokemon/news/where-to-find-dawn-stones-in-pokemon-scarlet-and-violet)

### 3. Clefairy → Clefable / 皮皮 → 皮可西

Switch the map to **Kitakami**, then fly to **Crystal Pool**, the water at the
summit of Oni Mountain. At night, search the mountain's nearby cave habitat;
Clefairy is listed for Crystal Pool and the surrounding Oni Mountain/Infernal
Pass areas. This is a search region, not a guaranteed Clefairy standing beside
the flight point. Scout and record one bounded cave route before automation.
The southeast side of Crystal Pool also has a drop-down cave entrance; verify
its Clefairy habitat and a recoverable ledge before adopting that particular
entrance. Avoid blindly dropping to the deepest chamber.

Pick up **TM098 Skill Swap southeast of the pond**, near the water's edge, if
it has not already been collected. Catch Clefairy and inspect its ability.
Use a Moon Stone to evolve it, then require Magic Guard in the final summary.
Cute Charm can be changed with an Ability Capsule from postgame Chansey Supply.

**Moon Stone route:** return to Paldea and fly to **Porto Marinada** on the west
coast. Visit the nearby lighthouse and inspect the fixed Moon Stone pickup next
to it. Check inventory first: one-time pickups may already have been taken.
Random mountain sparkles and auction inventory are fallbacks, not deterministic
substitutes. The exact lighthouse approach needs recording.

[Clefairy locations in both versions](https://www.serebii.net/pokedex-sv/clefairy/),
[night restrictions](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/List_of_nocturnal_Pok%C3%A9mon),
[Skill Swap pickup](https://www.thonky.com/pokemon-scarlet-violet/crystal-pool),
[Moon Stone pickup](https://www.serebii.net/pokearth/paldea/westprovinceareatwo.shtml),
[ability conversion](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Ability_Capsule)

### 4. Finish preparation in towns

- **Medali:** northwest-central Paldea. Fly to a town Center, enter the Treasure
  Eatery restaurant, and speak to the cook to change Gallade to Ghost Tera for
  50 Ghost shards. Verify the result in its summary.
- **Chansey Supply:** available in Mesagoza, Cascarrafa, Levincia and Montenevera.
  Buy the required Ability Capsule and training supplies after inventory audit.
- **Any Pokémon Center:** heal, restore PP and Tera charge, craft unlocked TMs,
  and set the fixed party/move order before the first encounter.

[Treasure Eatery](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Treasure_Eatery),
[Chansey Supply](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Chansey_Supply)

## Legendary routes: all 25

All rows are **level 70 except Kubfu, level 30**. Flight anchors below are our
route choices; the linked guides establish target landmarks. Each row still
needs an arrival screenshot, precise waypoint/height, camera reset, interaction
position and recovery recording. Do not interpret the order as one continuous
overworld trip: heal/save and fly to each anchor independently.

These endpoint landmarks are described by

| 闪电鸟 Zapdos | Poco Path Lighthouse | Climb the lighthouse; inspect the top platform. |

| 凤王 Ho-Oh | Alfornada | Fly east over the highlands; search the hill near the waterfall. |
| 固拉多 Groudon | Alfornada | Enter the west cave entrance; descend beneath town. See cave note below. |
| 雷公 Raikou | West Province (Area One) – Central | Ascend toward the mountain lake northwest of Cortondo; inspect its north shore. |
| 代拉基翁 Terrakion | West Province (Area One) – Central | Fly east toward crater-facing cliffs; use the marker map to select the correct shelf. |

**Groudon cave detail:** from the upper entrance west of Alfornada, reach the
main cavern, locate the raised Tera Umbreon platform, then search southeast of
it. A flight destination above the cave does not put you at the encounter's
height. This interior route needs separate ledge waypoints.

## Route recording priority

1. Team acquisition and shops, including resource audit and night handling.
2. Fury Falls → Kubfu: short route and the first full save/catch/resume trial.
3. Lighthouse → Zapdos: fixed landmark and an Electric/Soak trial.
4. A simple land encounter, then a healing encounter.
5. Solgaleo, both horses and weather-exposed Moltres battle validation.
6. Remaining surface flights, then sea and cave routes.

Keep per-route evidence and failure counts. A useful finished route includes
the map marker image/source, a video of the approach, named visual checkpoints,
bounded search region, altitude transitions, tested timings, and recovery back
to the starting flight point. None of those console measurements exist yet.
