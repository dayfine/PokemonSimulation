# Pokeguy LeafGreen: trainers and level targets by segment

Follow the route in video order, from the Lv5 Squirtle gift to the first
Champion win. **All 69 trainer battles are numbered once below**, with their
complete opposing parties and the starter's calculated level before → after.
Targets follow Pokeguy's documented two early wild wins and seven Rare Candies.
They describe the EXP route conditional on solo wins, not minimum safe levels
or battle win probabilities. Apart from those two early wins and the scripted
Tower Marowak, random wild encounters supply no EXP.

Numbers come from the [battle ledger](../../data/runs/pokeguy-leafgreen-15934/events.json)
and pinned pret trainer/species data using the [EXP calculator](README.md).
The [detailed audit](pokeguy-exp.md) includes cumulative EXP, video-note
comparisons, and the flee-all-random-wild alternative. Expected levels in this
guide are calculated; not every before/after level is a separate direct video
reading. Trainer links open the battle's approximate video start; Pokémon are
listed in defeat order. Squirtle evolves after Josh, and Wartortle evolves
before Hideout Giovanni.

## The early 39 EXP threshold

Defeat the Route 1 **Rattata Lv3 (24 EXP)** and **Pidgey Lv2 (15 EXP)**.
Together they bring Squirtle to Lv7 before Sammy, then Lv8 after Sammy.
Liam's Geodude raises it to Lv9; his Sandshrew raises it to **exactly
Lv11 / 742 EXP**, ready for Brock. Brock then leaves it at Lv13, where the
notes record learning Water Gun. This demonstrates the thresholds crossed;
it does not establish the runner's intent or that these levels guarantee wins.

| Checkpoint | Documented wild wins | Flee both wilds |
|---|---:|---:|
| Enter Sammy | Lv7 | Lv6 |
| Enter Liam | Lv8 | Lv7 |
| Enter Brock | Lv11 / 742 EXP | Lv10 / 703 EXP |
| Leave Brock | Lv13 / 1,286 EXP | Lv12 / 1,247 EXP |
| Leave Colton | Lv14 | Lv13 |
| Leave Greg | Lv15 / 2,096 EXP | Lv15 / 2,057 EXP |

The 39 EXP difference lasts until the first Cerulean Rare Candy. Both routes
are Lv18 before that Candy; it sets both to **Lv19 / 4,575 EXP**. The second
sets both to **Lv20 / 5,460 EXP before Misty**. All later EXP totals and level
targets in this guide are the same in both modes with the recorded Candy schedule.
